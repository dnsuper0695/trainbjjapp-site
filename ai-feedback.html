Help Train Our AI

Train BJJ includes three AI-powered features — the AI Training Coach, Post-Session Insights, and Notebook Scanner. Your feedback directly improves the quality of coaching advice for everyone. Every correction you send makes the AI smarter.

How It Works

1

Use the AI Features

Chat with the AI Coach, generate a Post-Session Insight after training, or scan your notebook to log a session.

2

Notice Something Off

Maybe the AI praised a submission you actually got caught by, suggested sweeps from top position, or missed a technique in your notes.

3

Send Us Feedback

Use the template below to tell us what happened and what the correct response should be. We use your feedback to improve the prompts.

What We're Looking For

These are the most common types of AI errors we want to fix.

⇵

Outcome Confusion

The AI treated a negative outcome as positive (or vice versa). For example, praising you for a submission you actually got caught by.

🚫

Position Logic Errors

The AI suggested something impossible for the position — like "work on sweeps from top" or "pass guard from bottom."

🧠

Missing BJJ Knowledge

The AI doesn't know enough about a specific technique, position, or concept to give useful advice.

🔍

Wrong Technique Suggestions

The AI suggested creating a technique that already exists in your library, or categorized a technique incorrectly.

📄

Format or Display Issues

The AI returned a malformed response, garbled text, or action buttons that didn't work correctly.
