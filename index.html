Deep Metric Breakdown

Drill into the numbers that matter. Offense compares submissions, sweeps, takedowns, escapes, and guard passes. Defense shows taps, times swept, taken down, and guard passed. Ratios like sub-to-tap, sweep ratio, and net score reveal who's actually more efficient.

  • ⚔️ Offense: submissions, sweeps, takedowns, escapes, and guard passes compared side by side
  • 🛡️ Defense: taps, got swept, got taken down, and got guard passed — see who's getting caught more
  • 📊 Ratios: sub-to-tap, sweep, takedown, guard pass, and overall net score for true efficiency comparison
  • 🏆 Percentile rankings from Needs Work to Elite show where each metric lands

Your Data

Train BJJ works with any fitness wearable that writes to Apple Health — WHOOP, Garmin, Polar, Apple Watch, Oura Ring, Fitbit, COROS, Suunto, and more. Heart rate, calories, and duration flow into every session automatically.

01

Health Data on Every Session

Heart rate, calories burned, and workout duration are pulled directly from Apple Health and attached to your training sessions. No manual entry — just train and your wearable handles the rest.

  • ♥ Average heart rate, calories, and duration shown on every session detail
  • ⌨️ Works with any device that syncs to Apple Health — WHOOP, Garmin, Polar, Apple Watch, Oura Ring, Fitbit, COROS, Suunto, and more
  • 📈 Health stats flow into your profile analytics and shareable session cards

Heart rate, calories, and duration on every session

Automatic Session Detection

Finish training and Train BJJ knows. The app monitors Apple Health for new workouts and prompts you to log a session — with the health data already attached. One tap and you're done.

  • 🔔 Workout Detected prompt appears automatically after training
  • ⏱️ Duration, calories, heart rate, and source device shown at a glance
  • 👉 Tap "Create Session" to log it instantly with all health data pre-filled

Auto-detected workout ready to log in one tap

Import Health History

Started using Train BJJ after months of training? No problem. Bulk import past workouts directly from Apple Health — choose a 3, 6, or 12 month window and bring your entire training history up to speed in seconds.

  • 📅 Pull martial arts, wrestling, and mixed cardio workouts from Apple Health
  • 📋 Select which workouts to import — see type, duration, and calories for each
  • ✅ Duplicate detection ensures workouts already in your journal aren't imported twice

Bulk import past workouts from Apple Health

HealthKit Matching for Every Import

However you bring data into Train BJJ, health metrics come along for the ride. CSV import, notebook scanner, and direct Health import all match your sessions to the closest HealthKit workout — so every session gets heart rate and calories attached.

  • 📄 CSV import matches each row to the closest HealthKit workout by time
  • 📷 Notebook scanner photos match to HealthKit workouts on the same date
  • 🔗 Toggle "Match with HealthKit workouts" on or off for any import method

Every import method enriches sessions with health data
