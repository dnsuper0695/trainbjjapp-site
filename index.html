Your Open Mat,
Fully Accounted For

Log your rounds, submissions, and taps as they happen — not hours later from memory. Built-in timers, one-tap stat tracking, and lock screen controls make it easy to capture everything between rounds.

01

Built-In Round Timer

Start the timer when you step on the mat. It runs in the background while you roll, and audio cues let you know when rounds start and end — so you never have to check your phone mid-roll.

  • ⏱ Configurable round timer with auto-roll to the next round
  • 🔢 See your running totals — rounds rolled, submissions hit, and taps given
  • 🔔 Audio cues for round start and end, even in silent mode

Round timer with real-time session stats

02

Quick Controls Between Rounds

When the round ends, grab your water and tap a few buttons. Increment your submissions, log a tap, tag the triangle you hit — it takes seconds, and you'll never forget the details.

  • 👉 Tap +/- to log rounds rolled, submissions, and taps as they happen
  • 🥇 Tag techniques you used — with position and outcome — while they're fresh
  • 🎯 Your focus points stay visible so you remember what to work on each round

Increment stats and tag techniques between rounds

03

Save & Reflect

When you're done rolling, everything you tracked is already filled in. Add your notes while the session is still fresh, then save — no more trying to reconstruct your rolls from memory at home.

  • 📝 Choose session type: Open Mat only, Class + Open Mat, or Class Before
  • ✍️ Add class notes and open mat reflections — what worked, what didn't
  • ⏱ Class Before adjusts your start time to account for instruction time

Save your session with class notes and reflections

04

Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

Don't want to unlock your phone between rounds? You don't have to. Increment your stats right from the lock screen or Dynamic Island — your phone never leaves your bag.

  • 📱 Tap +/- buttons on the lock screen to update rounds, submissions, and taps
  • 🟢 Dynamic Island shows your session duration and round count at a glance
  • 🔒 No unlocking, no app switching — just quick taps and back to rolling
